Our Specialty

Discover what makes dining at Aryan's an unforgettable experience for families, foodies, and celebrations.

Coastal Konkan

Seafood

Fresh coastal favourites including crispy Bombil Kurkure, tandoori Bangda, and masala prawns, prepared with aromatic Konkan spices and served with lime and chutney.

Dum Pukht Special

Murg Kheema Biryani

Aromatic basmati rice layered with spiced chicken kheema, caramelized onions, and warming biryani masala, then slow-cooked dum style and served with cooling raita.

Local Heritage

Maharashtrian Dish / Thali

Heirloom recipes featuring our legendary Kala Masala gravies, hearty rassa, and piping hot Jowar Bhakri made with authentic Pune spices.

Charcoal Grilled

Tandoor & Grills

Succulent kebabs, chicken lollipops, and whole fish char-grilled over flaming earthen tandoors, delivering rich smoky perfection.

Special Family Seating (Tree / Jungle View)

Dine amidst lush greenery with peaceful tree canopy views, comfortable breeze, and spacious tables tailored for family privacy.
